Profile

About

I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Science (Data Science) at AIUB, working on interpretable machine learning for graph-structured and biomedical data. My research spans complex network analysis, spatial transcriptomics, and clinical risk prediction, with a focus on methods that are accurate, data-efficient, and transparent to domain experts. I am seeking a PhD position to continue this work.

Research Interests

Machine Learning Interpretable AI Graph Neural Networks Complex Networks Spatial Transcriptomics Healthcare AI Visual Analytics

Direction

Research Focus

Three threads connect my work: methods that explain their predictions, methods that exploit graph structure, and methods that learn well from limited labels.

Interpretable ML for Healthcare

Domain-aware ensembles with SHAP explanations that expose the clinical, psychological, and social factors behind a risk prediction.

Postpartum depression risk (submitted); heart disease prediction (ICERECT 2025)

Graph Methods & Complex Networks

Direction-aware, BFS-based descriptors for analyzing, comparing, and visualizing real-world networks.

DCensus (submitted to Applied Network Science)

Label-Efficient Spatial Transcriptomics

Lightweight graph convolutional networks that identify spatial domains from scribble annotations and stay robust to label noise.

ScribbleST (manuscript in preparation)

Also: visual analytics for climate and environmental trends (Master’s thesis) and satellite-based analysis of dengue risk (ongoing).

Submitted to Springer Applied Network Science

We develop DCensus, a direction-aware graph analysis framework that employs BFS-based descriptors to analyze, compare, and visualize real-world complex networks.

View Paper →

“Disentangling Postpartum Depression Risk: A Domain-Aware Hierarchical Ensemble Approach for Interpretable Clinical Decision Support”

Submitted to Springer Nature Switzerland AG

This work proposes a domain-aware hierarchical stacking ensemble combining XGBoost, Logistic Regression, and SHAP to predict postpartum depression risk and to interpret the principal clinical, psychological, and social factors.

View Paper →

In Progress

“ScribbleST: Scribble-Supervised Spatial Domain Identification via Graph Convolutional Networks”

Manuscript in preparation, 2026

We develop ScribbleST, a lightweight graph convolutional network framework for spatial domain identification in spatial transcriptomics, evaluating annotation strategies and robustness to label noise to improve accuracy under minimal supervision.

View Paper →
